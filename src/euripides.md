# Quotes of Euripides

> "I have found power in the mysteries of thought." — Euripides

---

1. Time will explain it all. He is a talker, and needs no questioning before he speaks.
2. Waste not fresh tears over old griefs.
3. Whom the gods would destroy, they first make mad.
4. When one with honeyed words but evil mind persuades the mob, great woes befall the state.
5. Human misery must somewhere have a stop; there is no wind that always blows a storm.
6. Events will take their course, it is no good of being angry at them; he is happiest who wisely turns them to the best account.
7. I care for riches, to make gifts to friends or to repay my foes.
8. Question everything. Learn something. Answer nothing.
9. Cleverness is not wisdom.
10. The tongue is not steel, yet it cuts.
11. There is nothing lost by civility.
12. Leave no stone unturned.
13. Every man is like the company he is wont to keep.
14. I loathe a friend whose gratitude grows old, a friend who takes his friend's prosperity but will not voyage with him in his grief.
15. Happiness is brief. It will not stay. God batters at its sails.
16. Slight not what's near through aiming at what's far.
17. Talk is cheap. Silence is expensive.
18. He who overcomes his anger conquers his greatest enemy.
19. The man is happiest who lives from day to day and asks no more, garnering the simple goodness of life.
20. Who dares not speak his free thoughts is a slave.
21. There is just one life for each of us: our own.
22. No one can confidently say that he will still be living tomorrow.
23. Youth is the best time to be rich, and the best time to be poor.
24. There is in the worst of fortune the best of chances for a happy change.
25. Knowledge is indeed better than blind practice.
26. You were a stranger to sorrow: therefore Fate has cursed you.
27. What greater grief than the loss of one's native land?
28. One loyal friend is worth ten thousand relatives.
29. Danger gleams like sunshine to a brave man's eyes.
30. In case of dissension, never dare to judge till you've heard the other side.
31. Fortune truly helps those who are of good judgment.
32. Talk sense to a fool and he calls you foolish.
33. The good and the wise lead quiet lives.
34. A bad beginning makes a bad ending.
35. Love is all we have, the only way that each can help the other.
36. The best of seers is he who guesses well.
37. The fiercest anger of all, the most incurable, is that which rages in the place of dearest love.
38. We know the good, we apprehend it clearly, but we can't bring it to achievement.
39. Stronger than lover's love is lover's hate. Incurable, in each, the wounds they make.
